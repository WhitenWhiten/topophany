Connect to a collector

Read-only. Your token goes straight to the collector and is never saved.

Connection troubleshooting
  1. Check that this machine can reach the collector address and that its service is running.
  2. Use the project ID from your topology and a viewer token; worker tokens cannot open this view.
  3. Browsers may hide whether a failure is DNS, certificate trust, or cross-origin access. A working terminal connection alone does not verify browser access.

For this hosted viewer, allow this exact origin on the collector:

--web-origin https://topophany.com

With the matching saved collector profile, generate a local diagnostic report:

topo doctor --json
